Fructan guide — onion, garlic & wheat

A focused companion to the general allergen guide, for anyone limiting fructans specifically — the FODMAP group found heavily in onion, garlic, shallot, leek, and wheat/gluten grains.

Bringing your own bread is a genuinely good call — see the wheat breakdown below, it's in almost everything baked or doughy this week.

And to be direct about the harder part: onion and/or garlic is cooked into the shared base of all three main dinners this week (Pasta, Curry, Wraps) — before it ever splits into vegetarian or meat portions. There's no "pick it out" option for those the way there is with, say, a cheese topping. The realistic paths are: a separate small batch cooked without it (needs Vera/kitchen crew to plan for), bringing/prepping your own for those nights, or leaning on the genuinely clean options called out below.

Quick answer

🧅🧄 The 3 cooked dinners

Tue Pasta, Wed Curry, and Thu Wraps all have onion and/or garlic cooked into the shared base before serving — confirmed on the product labels, not a guess. No portion of any of these three is onion/garlic-free as currently planned.

🌾 Wheat is everywhere

Pasta, pita, tortillas, baguette, apple pie, biscuits, cookies, cake, and the Saturday salted mix are all wheat-based. Bringing your own bread/crackers covers a lot of ground.

✅ What's actually clean

The raw veg plate on Thursday (carrot/cucumber/pepper, skip the hummus), plain & paprika chips on Saturday, plain cheeses on Wednesday's board (not the dip), and most of the all-week fruit bowl.

🍎 One more thing labels can't show

Fresh whole fruit (like apple and watermelon) can be naturally high in fructose/fructans regardless of any ingredient label — that's outside what this page can check. Worth factoring in separately if you're being strict.

All-week fruit bowl

No onion, garlic, or wheat on any label — but this is exactly the category where whole-fruit fructan content matters most. Watermelon and apples (both in the bowl) are commonly flagged as higher-FODMAP fresh fruit; grapes, oranges, clementines, bananas, and pears are generally easier. Worth checking against whatever guidance your dietitian gave you rather than this label-based page.

This page checks packaged-product ingredient labels (pulled 2026-07-20) for onion, garlic, shallot, leek, and wheat — it can't verify fresh spice blends, "specerijen" catch-all ingredients, or natural FODMAP content in whole fruit/vegetables. It's a starting point for a conversation with Vera, not a medical guarantee.
